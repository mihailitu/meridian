# Strategy Loss Diagnostics — 2026-07-18/19

**Purpose.** The phase-3/5 IS/OOS runs earned every strategy a DEAD verdict. This report
asks *why* each one loses — diagnosis and hypothesis generation, **not** verification.
Nothing here re-opens a verdict; anything promising must go through a fresh IS tuning pass
and a single OOS shot per the gate discipline.

**Method.** Per-trade data from the certified runs was not preserved (the fulltest DB is
wiped per run; JSON sidecars are aggregate-only), so fills were regenerated by re-running
each strategy **isolated** over the certified windows (IS 2024-08-01→2025-08-01, OOS
2025-08-01→2026-02-01; narrowed universe AAPL MSFT GOOGL AMZN NVDA; pairs on AAPL/MSFT;
discovery on only for discovery_momentum). Round trips paired with the certified
`pair_fills_fifo`; regime features computed offline from parquet (the pipeline's own regime
labels are degenerate on 1m data — audit P2-11, empirically confirmed below).

**Caveats.** (1) Isolated runs remove cross-strategy cash competition and the shared
daily-loss halt, so trade counts exceed the certified runs (e.g. mean_reversion 1,465 IS
round trips vs 167 recorded) — better samples for diagnosis, but not the certified
measurement. (2) Fidelity checks: pairs regenerated **exactly** the recorded iteration-10
gross P&L (IS −$36,899 / OOS −$20,569); the momentum gate replication reproduced the
recorded 0 IS / 1 OOS trades. (3) PaperBroker fills at tick price with $1/fill commission;
no spread/slippage model — real-world costs would be worse, which only strengthens the
conclusions below.

---

## Cross-cutting findings

1. **Three of six strategies lose by construction, not by market verdict.**
   - `momentum`: entry gate `trend_strength > 30` uses a formula whose scale is daily but
     is computed on 1m bars (median strength during bullish bars ≈ 0.8, p99 ≈ 7).
     Result: **zero entries in a year × 5 symbols** (1 in OOS). The strategy was never
     actually tested.
   - `multi_timeframe`: entry (pullback to within 0.3% of the 1m SMA20 while the 5m trend
     flag is up) and exit (market-close the instant one 5m close crosses below the same
     trend SMA) trigger at essentially the same price with zero hysteresis → 1m noise
     flips ~every position out at ~−0.15% within ~20m. The 5–30m hold bucket has a
     **0.4–0.5% win rate over ~4,500 trades**; trades that escape 2h+ win 65–68% and are
     net **positive +$12k in both legs**.
   - `pairs`: orders only ever touch `symbol_a` — an unhedged long-AAPL bet driven by a
     ratio z-score. Ratio reversion doesn't require AAPL to rise (MSFT falling also
     reverts it), so signal and position are mismatched: 13–17% WR, **all 18 months
     negative**, losers' median MFE ≈ 0.
2. **Costs are not the story.** Commission is 6–14% of net losses everywhere; the losses
   are signal/exit-structure losses. (Real spread costs would add to marginal cases like
   overnight_reversal OOS.)
3. **Exit discipline is the recurring soft failure.** mean_reversion has no time stop and
   its losers were never in profit; overnight_reversal's losers *were* in profit intraday
   (median MFE ~0.3%) and gave it back; multi_timeframe's positive long-hold bucket is
   strangled by its own trend-flip exit.
4. **Extended-hours entries lose consistently** (mean_reversion 4–7am ET: WR ~25%, all
   negative in both legs). No strategy gates on session time.
5. **P2-11 confirmed empirically**: volatility percentile on 1m bars has median ~1.2,
   never sustains ≥60 with strength >70 → BREAKOUT/BREAKDOWN occur on exactly 0.0% of
   ~1.4M bars. Every regime branch conditioned on them is dead code, and vol-conditioned
   behavior generally is inert.

## Per-strategy detail

### momentum — "never tested" (gate scaling bug)
Offline replication of all four entry gates on IS+OOS (10 symbol-legs, ~1.4M bars):
regime gate passes ~31% of bars, SMA gate ~50%, RSI-cross ~6.7%, but
`trend_strength > 30` passes 0.01–0.04% and never coincides with the rest → co-fire 0 (IS),
1 (OOS, AMZN — the recorded +$65 trade). Without the strength gate there would be
~2,800–3,600 candidate entries per symbol-year. **Hypothesis worth a gated test:** rescale
the threshold to the 1m distribution (e.g. strength > p90-of-bullish ≈ 2–4) or compute
strength on higher-timeframe closes.

**Rescale outcome (2026-07-18, IS-only, pre-registered S ∈ {2,3,4}):** the idea finally
trades (150–667 entries/yr) and is **negative at every threshold even at zero slippage**
(PF 0.60–0.78; median hold 7–12m — entries buy short spikes the bearish-flip/RSI-70 exits
unwind within minutes; higher S = more extended spikes = worse). No edge was hiding
behind the broken gate. Retired.

### mean_reversion — knife-catching entries, no time stop
IS: 1,465 trips, net −$21.3k, PF 0.41, WR 36%. OOS: 747 trips, −$15.6k, PF 0.29.
Losses concentrate in holds >30m (IS: −$22.2k across 30m–1d buckets at ~19–25% WR);
sub-30m holds ≈ breakeven (+$1.4k IS / −$1.4k OOS — inconsistent, not a claimable edge).
Losers' median MFE 0.07–0.08% vs median MAE −0.33%: entries at the lower band + RSI<35
catch falling prices that never bounce; exits (upper band / RSI>70 / −1.5% stop) leave no
early bail-out. High-vol days lose 2.4× low-vol; 4–7am ET entries uniformly negative.
**Hypotheses:** time stop (~30m), session filter, vol-regime filter — diagnosis suggests
they remove loss buckets, but the remaining "edge" bucket is IS/OOS-inconsistent, so
expectations should be low.

### multi_timeframe — whipsaw churn (zero-hysteresis exit)
IS: 4,922 trips, net −$96.2k, PF 0.19. OOS: 4,035 trips, −$92.3k, PF 0.21. Structure as in
cross-cutting #1. The stop (2%) almost never fires — avg loser is −0.15%, i.e. the
trend-flip exit. **Hypotheses:** exit hysteresis (flip only below SMA×(1−b)), or
trend-flip exit only when in profit, or entries away from the trend boundary. The 65–68%
WR / +$12k long-hold bucket in *both* legs makes this the most promising re-design
candidate of the narrow strategies.

**Redesign outcome (2026-07-18, IS-only research layer, pre-registered variants):** the
mechanism fix works — hysteresis / profit-gated flip exits cut churn 14.2k→3.7–5.6k
trades/yr and lift frictionless PF 0.92→1.02–1.05 — but the surviving edge is ~$2/trade
(~1bp on an $18k position). Under the platform's cost model (10bps slippage/side +
commission; fills confirmed at exactly ±10bps of bar close) every variant is deeply
negative (PF ≤ 0.60), and even at a realistic-for-megacaps 2bps/side every variant still
loses (avg −$6 to −$7.5/trade). No variant passes the pre-registered selection rule, so
nothing graduates to the fulltest gate. **Conclusion: the recorded PF 0.19 was cost
amplification of churn; beneath it the entry signal is ~zero-edge at 1m horizon. DEAD
stands — now for the right reason.** Corollary: the recorded catastrophe decomposes as
≈$0 signal + ~$38/round-trip platform costs × extreme churn; intraday 1m strategies on
this platform must clear a ~3–5bp/trade hurdle even under generous cost assumptions.

### pairs — signal/position mismatch (not actually a pairs trade)
IS: 2,156 trips, net −$41.2k, PF 0.16. OOS: 1,167 trips, −$22.9k, PF 0.14 (gross matches
recorded iteration-10 numbers exactly). Uniform ~−0.25%/trade drift, every month negative,
no tail concentration (top-10 worst = 5–6% of losses), losers' median MFE ≈ 0.
**Hypotheses:** either trade the actual spread (long A / short B — requires short support
in OMS/strategies) or retire the strategy; parameter tuning cannot fix a one-legged hedge.

### overnight_reversal — tail-gap asymmetry, no profit-taking
IS: 284 trips, net −$9.4k, PF 0.47, WR 45%. OOS: 139 trips, net ≈ 0 (PF 0.98), WR 52%.
The certified "OOS PF 2.85" was a 25-trade fluke — at 5.6× the sample it evaporates.
Losers average 1.7× winners; top-10 worst trades are 32–37% of gross losses (big gap-downs
that keep falling — tail is momentum, not reversion). Losers' median MFE ~0.3%: profits
existed intraday and were given back (exit is time-based only). **Hypotheses:** skip
entries after extreme gaps (tail filter), intraday take-profit. Closest-to-alive of the
six on OOS evidence, but IS says the base signal is still negative.

**Redesign outcome (2026-07-18, IS-only, pre-registered tail filters {3,4,5}% +
pre-market take-profits {0.5,1.0}%):** baseline replication validated (PF 0.47 exactly
matches the regenerated run). Every variant negative even at zero slippage (baseline PF
0.69; best variant, tp=0.5%, PF 0.75); the tail filter barely helps because the deep-loss
band also contains winners. The IS-year close-to-open reversal on these mega-caps is a
negative-drift signal; the certified OOS PF 2.85 (25 trades) and the isolated OOS
breakeven were regime luck. Retired.

## Implications (for discussion, not action)

- The "no edge exists here" phase-3 conclusion needs a footnote: for momentum,
  multi_timeframe, and pairs the harness measured **broken implementations**, not absent
  edges. The verdicts stand for those implementations; they say nothing about the ideas.
- Any revival goes through the gate: one IS tuning pass, one OOS shot, pre-registered.
- If regime-conditioned strategies are ever wanted, P2-11 must be fixed first (empirical
  percentile or frequency-scaled annualization); today vol-conditioning is inert.
- A vectorized research layer over parquet (pandas) did all of the above in minutes per
  experiment; the fulltest pipeline should be reserved for final verification runs.
